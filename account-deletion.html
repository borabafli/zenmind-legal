🗑️ Account Deletion Guide

Last Updated: January 21, 2025

⚠️ Warning: Permanent Action

Deleting your ZenMind account is permanent and cannot be undone. This action will:

  • Permanently delete your account and login credentials
  • Remove all your data including chat sessions, mood ratings, thought patterns, goals, and values
  • Cancel any active subscriptions
  • Erase all your insights, journals, and exercises

There is no way to recover your data after deletion.

Who Can Delete Their Account?

Only registered users (those who signed up with email, Apple Sign In, or social media) can delete their accounts.

If you're using ZenMind as a guest (anonymous user), you don't have an account to delete. You can only delete your local data instead.

How to Delete Your Account

1

Open ZenMind App

Launch the ZenMind mobile application on your iOS or Android device.

2

Go to Your Profile

Tap on your profile icon or navigate to the Profile section of the app.

3

Access Data & Privacy Settings

In your Profile, look for and tap on "Your Data & Privacy" or "Data & Privacy" menu option.

4

Tap "Delete Account"

Scroll down to find the "Delete Account" button (displayed with a red/warning color). Tap on it.

Note: This button will be disabled if you're using the app as a guest user.

5

Review What Will Be Deleted

The app will show you a summary of all data that will be permanently deleted, including:

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Number of chat sessions
  • Number of thought patterns
  • Number of mood ratings
  • Number of goals and values
  • Total items to be removed
6

Confirm by Typing "DELETE"

To prevent accidental deletion, you'll be asked to type "DELETE" (in capital letters) to confirm.

Type DELETE exactly as shown and tap the confirm button.

7

Account Deletion Process

The app will:

  1. Delete all your local data from your device
  2. Remove your account from our authentication system
  3. Clean up any server-side data associated with your account
  4. Cancel any active subscriptions

This process usually takes a few seconds.

8

Account Deleted Successfully

Once complete, you'll see a confirmation message. You'll be automatically signed out, and your account will be permanently deleted.

You can create a new account anytime if you wish to use ZenMind again.

What Happens After Deletion?

  • Immediate: Your account is deactivated and you're signed out
  • Within 24 hours: All your data is permanently removed from our systems
  • Subscriptions: Any active subscriptions are cancelled (no further charges)
  • Data Recovery: Your data cannot be recovered after deletion

Alternative: Delete Data Only

Don't want to delete your account?

If you want to keep your account but remove all your data (chat history, moods, insights, etc.), you can use the "Delete My Data" feature instead.
