🗂️ Data Deletion Guide

Last Updated: January 21, 2025

What's the difference?

Delete My Data: Removes all your personal data (chat history, moods, insights, etc.) but keeps your account active. You can continue using ZenMind with a fresh start.

Delete Account: Permanently deletes both your data AND your account/login credentials. You'll need to create a new account to use ZenMind again.

This guide covers "Delete My Data" only. To delete your entire account, see the Account Deletion Guide.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

When you use "Delete My Data," the following information will be permanently removed from your device:

  • Chat Sessions: All conversations with Anu (our AI companion)
  • Thought Patterns: Your recorded thoughts and reflections
  • Mood Ratings: All mood check-ins and emotional tracking data
  • Goals: Personal goals you've set
  • Values: Core values you've identified
  • Exercises: Completed mindfulness and wellness exercises
  • Journal Entries: Personal journal entries and notes
  • Insights: AI-generated insights and recommendations

What's NOT deleted: Your account credentials (email/login), subscription status, and account settings.

⚠️ Important: This Action Cannot Be Undone

Once you delete your data:

  • All your data is permanently removed from your device
  • There is no backup - we don't store your sensitive data on our servers
  • Data cannot be recovered - this deletion is permanent
  • You'll start with a completely fresh slate in the app

Who Can Delete Their Data?

Everyone! Both registered users and guest users (anonymous) can delete their data. This feature is available to all ZenMind users.

How to Delete Your Data

1

Open ZenMind App

Launch the ZenMind mobile application on your iOS or Android device.

2

Go to Your Profile

Tap on your profile icon or navigate to the Profile section of the app.

3

Access Data & Privacy Settings

In your Profile, look for and tap on "Your Data & Privacy" or "Data & Privacy" menu option.

4

Tap "Delete My Data"

Scroll down to find the "Delete My Data" button. Tap on it.

Note: This is different from "Delete Account" - make sure you select the correct option!

5

Review Data Summary

The app will show you exactly what will be deleted, including:

  • Number of chat sessions
  • Number of thought patterns
  • Number of mood ratings
  • Number of goals
  • Number of values
  • Total number of items to be removed

Example: "You're about to delete all your data: • 15 chat sessions • 8 thought patterns • 23 mood ratings • 3 goals • 5 values. Total: 54 items. This action cannot be undone."

6

Confirm First Time

Tap "Continue" to proceed to the final confirmation.

7

Final Confirmation

You'll see a second confirmation dialog asking "Are You Sure?"

The message will remind you: "This will permanently delete all your insights, moods, goals, and chat history. This cannot be undone."

Tap "Delete Everything" to confirm.

8

Deletion Process

The app will:

  1. Show a "Deleting Data" progress indicator
  2. Remove all items from your device storage
  3. Clear all cached data

This process usually takes just a few seconds.

9

Data Deleted Successfully

You'll see a success message confirming what was deleted:

"Successfully deleted all your data: • X chat sessions • X thought patterns • X mood ratings • Plus exercises, journals, goals, values, and more. Total: X items removed"

Tap "OK" to return to the app with a fresh start.

What Happens After Data Deletion?

  • Immediate: All your data is removed from your device
  • Account Status: Your account remains active and logged in
  • Subscription: Your subscription status is unchanged
  • Fresh Start: You can immediately start using ZenMind again with empty history
  • No Recovery: Deleted data cannot be restored

Privacy & Data Storage

How ZenMind Handles Your Data:

  • Local Storage: All your sensitive data (chat history, moods, journals) is stored locally on your device
  • No Server Backups: We don't keep backups of your personal data on our servers
  • AI Processing: When you chat with Anu, messages are sent securely to our AI service only to generate responses in real-time. They are not stored permanently.
  • Encryption: Your data is encrypted both in transit and at rest on your device
  • Complete Control: You own your data and can delete it anytime

Learn more in our Privacy Policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I recover my data after deletion?

No. Once deleted, your data is permanently removed and cannot be recovered. We don't maintain backups of your personal data on our servers.

Will deleting data cancel my subscription?

No. Deleting your data does not affect your subscription status. You'll remain subscribed to ZenMind Premium (if applicable) and can continue using all premium features.

What's the difference between "Delete My Data" and "Delete Account"?

Delete My Data: Removes all your personal content but keeps your account active. You can continue using ZenMind with the same login.

Delete Account: Permanently deletes both your data AND your account. You'll be signed out and need to create a new account to use ZenMind again.

Can I delete specific items instead of everything?

Currently, the "Delete My Data" feature removes all your data at once. If you want to delete specific items (like individual chat sessions or mood entries), you can do so from within those respective sections of the app.

Does this affect other devices?

Data deletion only affects the device you're using. Since most data is stored locally on your device, data on other devices (if you've logged in elsewhere) won't be automatically deleted.
